Frequently Asked Questions

Can Surfer help me with AI visibility?

Of course! Surfer is the most complete platform for AI Search Optimization and visibility.

  • AI Tracker helps you track, measure, and improve your AI visibility with clear insights on your Visibility Score, mention gaps, competitor share of voice, and more.
  • Topical Map helps you research and plan new content clusters designed to increase your topical authority and position you as an expert in your niche, thereby enhancing your chances of being cited and mentioned.
  • Content Audit helps you monitor your content's performance, notifies you about ranking drops so you can act fast, and suggests articles with the best quick-win potential to refresh (the fresher your content, the higher its chances of being mentioned and cited).
  • Content Editor's live guidelines help you write new and refresh existing content in a way that is rewarded both in SERPs and AI chats.

How does Surfer work?

Surfer makes SEO an accessible and predictable way to grow a business by delivering smart solutions that bring organic traffic. We provide a clear pathway to smarter, simpler SEO growth for agencies, teams, and marketers.

Our toolkit allows agencies, in-house teams, and SEOs to automate tasks around content management and drive more traffic in a cost-efficient way. Our platform was developed by a team of engineers, marketers, and SEO experts to make SEO content creation simple, efficient, and accessible.

We use NLP solutions, machine learning, an analysis of over 500 web signals, the newest AI technology, and more. All guidelines are real-time, competition-based, and Google-compliant.

Since our algorithms rely on reverse engineering, users trust the suggestions we make. Our metric, the Content Score, is the gold standard in the industry. We take the guesswork out of on-page SEO. Our users trust our tool to offer relevant, data-backed suggestions. SEO, simplified.

For more information, please visit our Knowledge Base.

Who uses Surfer?

Surfer is used by agencies, in-house teams, and individuals who want to power up their content marketing strategy and skyrocket organic traffic. Among them are well-known brands, such as Bolt, FedEx, ClickUp, Lenovo, Opera, Square, Shopify, FreshBooks and, hopefully, you! 🤞

What languages does Surfer support?

Surfer works with most languages! A full list of supported languages is available in our Knowledge Base.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my account after purchase?

Absolutely! You can upgrade or downgrade your subscription from the in-app billing section under the Change Plan button at any time. For assistance, feel free to reach out to us via chat or at [email protected]!

What is your cancellation policy?

To minimize the hassle on your side, we automatically renew your plan for each new billing period.

You can turn off the auto-renewal feature at any point via the in-app Billing page.

This will not result in an immediate closure of your account or its deletion - we simply won’t renew your ongoing plan for the next billing period. You'll have access to the app until the end of the already-paid billing period, regardless of the exact date you cancel.

In case of any issues with your renewal or cancellation, please reach out to the Support team via chat or email. You can also find detailed instructions in our guide.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit and debit cards (monthly & yearly subscriptions), and bank transfers (yearly subscriptions only). We do not support PayPal payments at the moment.

Can I purchase more Surfer AI credits?

Yes, you can always buy additional AI credits on demand. For the best value per AI credit, we recommend choosing plan Scale or Enterprise.

If you’re not ready for a recurring commitment or need more credits right away, you can top-up any active subscription with our AI packages (different sizes available inside the app).

If I have the annual plan, do my credits reset yearly or monthly?

Your credits reset yearly or after changing your plan (upgrades and downgrades).

If I choose the annual plan, do I have to pay upfront for the entire year?

Yes. Upon finalizing the purchase, you’ll be charged for the entire year. Remember: choosing the annual plan gives you a discount of up to 17% in comparison to the monthly payments. Don’t miss out!
