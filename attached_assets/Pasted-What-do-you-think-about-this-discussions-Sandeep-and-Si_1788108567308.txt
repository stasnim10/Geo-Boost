What do you think about this discussions: Sandeep and Simum discussed pivoting their business strategy toward an AI-driven decision-making tool for B2B companies.

Market Insights

Sandeep shared observations from a recent trip to San Francisco, noting that many YC-funded startups focus on simplifying existing solutions or targeting niche markets.





He noted GTM startups using AI models to provide competitor insights and market targeting data.



He observed specialized insurance companies like Kurgi focusing on tech niches, and chip manufacturers replicating large-scale computing hardware at lower costs.



At Founders Inc., he saw interest in physical AI and robotics designed to reduce idle time in manufacturing processes.

Product Strategy

The participants discussed moving away from simple information retrieval toward a tool that assists with high-level business decisions.





Instead of just providing data via tools like ChatGPT, the goal is to create a model that helps companies decide how to execute and predicts potential growth or failure branches.



The core value proposition focuses on helping businesses decide faster and work more efficiently by providing multiple outcomes for specific decisions.

Decision Making Challenges: A significant blocker identified is the acquisition of high-quality B2B scale data required to train such a model. Sandeep emphasized the need to research if Palantir has already dominated this space with their existing data infrastructure.

Research Tasks

The discussion concluded with immediate areas for investigation before proceeding to build an MVP for fundraising.





Simum will research existing decision-making frameworks and identify potential points of failure for their proposed idea.



Questions remain regarding how to obtain millions of data points necessary for training the model effectively without relying solely on manual human intervention or expensive training hours for robots.



Will the team be able to find a way to become "data merchants" or if they should focus strictly on selling products directly to businesses?
