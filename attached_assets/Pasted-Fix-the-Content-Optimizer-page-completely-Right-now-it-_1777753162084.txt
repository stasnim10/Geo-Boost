## Why The Waitlist Button Matters

That `/upgrade` page does something really important before you build anything. Every person who clicks **"Want us to publish this automatically?"** and fills out the waitlist form is telling you:

- They want the Done For You tier
- Which CMS platform they use (tells you which MCP to build first)
- Their email so you can sell to them directly

When you have 10 waitlist signups you have enough signal to decide whether to build WordPress MCP, Shopify MCP, or Google Business Profile first. You build exactly what your customers asked for instead of guessing.

That's how you use the MCP idea correctly — validate first, build second.
